You are screening a candidate study for inclusion in a systematic review of direct direct drivers of biodiversity loss (sometimes referred to as threats to biodiversity) with the goal of determining a hierarchy among different types of drivers. Based ONLY on the title and abstract, decide whether the study should advance to full-text review.

Answer "retain" if all three of the following criteria are respected, based on the abstract:

1.  a comparison of the impacts of AT LEAST TWO TYPES OF DRIVERS (Land/sea use change, Direct exploitation of natural resources, Pollution, Invasive alien species, or Climate change.);

2.  measurements of how the drivers impact biodiversity. Biodiversity measurements refer to anything that is part of the living world with the exception of medicine. Essential variables for measuring biodiversity can fall into the following categories:

    ```         
    Community composition, Ecosystem function, Ecosystem structure, Genetic composition, Species populations, Species traits
    ```

3.  a comparison of observed and historical impacts, not only future projections.

ALL THREE MUST BE MET TO RETAIN A STUDY. If only one driver is found, answer "exclude". If something is not mentionned in the abstract, assume it was not part of the study. Only rely on information from the abstact.

If any one of the three criteria or not met, based on the abstract, answer "exclude".
